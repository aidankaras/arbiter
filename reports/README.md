# Research log

One report per week, generated automatically and committed here.

Each covers events processed, per-arm performance against the live and shadow
books, where the arms disagreed and who was right, a walkthrough of
representative decisions including their reasoning, changes merged that week
with their benchmark deltas, cost against budget, and what is not working.

Reports are generated from the prediction and evaluation tables. Every figure in
a report traces to a query, and results that did not work are reported alongside
those that did.
